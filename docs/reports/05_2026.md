# Does it show up in 2026? (Aug–Oct 2026)

In 56 days of 2026 fills, 13.7% of eligible wallets pass the sign test after Benjamini–Hochberg correction. A label that covers one active wallet in seven isn't picking out a small informed group.

The fills come from the Hugging Face release, Aug 10 – Oct 4, 2026, and go through the same test as [report 1](01_stage1_skill_test.md). Three things differ from 2024. Markets are kept by their own end date, because the release has no parent-event dates. Sports and weather markets aren't filtered out, because it has no category tags. And taker fees are now about 1% of volume, where in 2024 they were close to zero.

| Taker volume | $2.75B | |

| Sign test p < 0.05 | 12,200 (16.1%) | 914 (8.0%) |
| Survive BH, q = 0.05 | 10,410 (13.7%) | 484 (4.2%) |

The 10,410 are 4.3% of all takers. Some of the gap with 2024 may come from the looser market rules above.

## What went wrong first

The first run used 13.5 days (Sep 21 – Oct 4) and flagged 22% of eligible wallets. Gamma pages cached while markets were still open had no payout. The sign test turned those missing profits into p = 0 and labelled 261 wallets Skilled, 53 of them with fewer than five events, which can't happen at p < 0.05.

The run above makes three fixes:
- it keeps a market only when every outcome has a payout and the payouts sum to 1;
- it refuses to score a wallet whose all-buy draw doesn't reproduce its actual PnL;
- it excludes wallets with fewer than five events.

## Do the 2024 winners keep winning?

This was only measured on the 13.5-day window. Of the 484 Skilled wallets from 2024, 20 traded at all, 3 had enough events to be retested, and 1 was flagged again, with a PnL of +$0.29. A second re-flag was a p = 0 artifact. The window is too short for a firm answer, but nothing here suggests that copying 2024 wallets would work.
